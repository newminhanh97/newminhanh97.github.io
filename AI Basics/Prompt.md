## What is a prompt?

A prompt is the question or instruction you type or share with an AI model to get a response.

## How to prompt effectively

Include the following details in your prompt:

- **Role**: Define the role the AI should take.
- **Context**: Provide the relevant background information.
- **Task**: Clearly describe what you want the AI to do.
- **Constraint**: Explain the rules to follow and what not to do.
  - What rules to follow
  - What NOT to do
- **Output**: Specify the format or result you expect.
